A few things
worth knowing.

A browser is a big thing to build.
Here’s where Crossbow stands.

Can I download Crossbow yet?

Not yet. Crossbow is a development prototype, and individual workflows are still being tested. The waitlist is for news about early access, not an immediate download or a guaranteed release date.

Which platforms are coming?

macOS is the first development target. Windows and Linux are planned, but neither has a verified release. Crossbow’s desktop interface is built in Rust, with Chromium through CEF as its web engine.

Is this related to Arc?

Crossbow is an independent project, inspired by sidebar-first browsing. It isn’t made by or affiliated with Arc or The Browser Company. Familiar workflows are the goal; full feature parity is not a claim.

Will I be able to bring my tabs?

Arc and Chrome importers are in development. Their scope and reliability are still being verified. Passwords, cookies and extensions are not included. You should keep your existing browser and backups.

Does joining create a browser account?

No. The email waitlist is separate from the browser’s account and sync service. Joining doesn’t create a browser session or transfer browsing data. We use Clerk to manage waitlist entries and Cloudflare to host and protect this site.

What should I know before switching?

Crossbow isn’t ready to replace your everyday browser yet. Chrome extensions are not currently supported. Some streaming services and provider sign-ins may not work with the embedded web engine. Early-access limitations will be made clear before a public download.
