Waitlist privacy.

Your email is for Crossbow early-access news. Joining the waitlist does not create a browser account, connect your existing browser or upload your browsing history.

Waitlist signup remains disabled until its email service, security checks and privacy contact are configured.

What you choose to share

The signup form asks for your email address. If the waitlist is open and your request is accepted, that address is sent to Clerk, our waitlist provider. Clerk records the entry and its status. A successful confirmation on the form means the provider accepted the entry; it does not guarantee an invitation or release date.

We use the address for early-access invitations and occasional Crossbow development updates. Your signup is consent to those emails. The waitlist does not ask for a password, payment details or browsing data.

Services involved

  • Clerk manages waitlist entries. See Clerk’s privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and processes requests. Its Turnstile service protects signup from automated abuse and may process device and network information. Rate limiting uses request network information. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

These services may process information in countries outside your own. Their published privacy terms describe their processing and safeguards.

Storage and security

The site does not save your email in localStorage, sessionStorage or browser cookies. The form holds the address while you enter and submit it, and clears it after a confirmed registration. The application does not write submitted email addresses to its logs. Cloudflare and Clerk may keep operational or security records under their own policies.

This landing page does not include advertising, retargeting or analytics scripts. The security check is loaded only when signup is enabled.

Keeping or removing your entry

We keep waitlist entries while running early access and sending the updates you requested, unless you ask us to remove yours. You can withdraw consent and ask for access, correction or removal using the contact below. Removal requests are handled by the Crossbow team; there is no self-service account created by this form.

A verified privacy contact must be configured before the waitlist can collect addresses. If no contact is shown here, signup should remain unavailable.

About the browser

This notice covers the website’s email waitlist only. Browser accounts, sync and any future product services have a separate scope. The browser is in development; joining this list does not enable those services.
